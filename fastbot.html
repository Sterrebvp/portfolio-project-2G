Fastbot — A Fastned Concept

Concept Development & 3D Visualisation

November 2025 · School project

Back to work ↗

For a school assignment, I chose Fastned and explored how a new product could attract more customers. I developed Fastbot: a mobile robot concept that delivers snacks and drinks to drivers while their cars charge, with orders placed through the Fastned app.

I created the robot concept, an app ordering screen, promotional posters and 3D visualisations.

Tools Twinmotion · SketchUp · Adobe Illustrator · Adobe Photoshop · Adobe InDesign
