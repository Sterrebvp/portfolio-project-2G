My “Temporary” Addiction

Campaign Concept & Interview Filming November 2025
01 — PROJECT

Concept & Contribution

November 2025 · Four-day international student collaboration

During a four-day project with German students, we were asked to develop a campaign around a Sustainable Development Goal. We chose Good Health and Well-being, focusing on young people’s views on smoking and whether they see themselves quitting in the future.

I developed the initial concept for My “Temporary” Addiction and pitched it together with two classmates. The German students then chose which project groups to join, and we worked together to develop the idea further.

My contribution included developing the initial concept, preparing the interview questions and filming the interview.
